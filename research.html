Research

Historical depth. Contemporary application.

STOPAZ connects scholarship with practical analysis so institutions can identify how antizionist narratives are formed, circulated, and normalized.

Research areas

From ideological genealogy to institutional response.

Genealogy

Trace Soviet, post-colonial, nationalist, religious, and institutional lineages.

Narrative systems

Examine libels, moral inversions, ideological conditioning, and recurring villain-victim frameworks.

Institutional spread

Study how antizionist ideas enter education, media, culture, professional life, and civic institutions.

Strategic response

Translate analysis into language, standards, training, and tools that institutions can use.

Current focus

The language that precedes the evidence.

Research examines how vocabulary, framing, and repeated narratives shape what audiences perceive before facts are evaluated.

Outputs

Research designed to travel.

Briefing papers and analytical reportsInstitutional guidance and terminologyCurriculum and teaching materialsLectures, panels, and public educationExhibition interpretation and visual archives
