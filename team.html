Founder

Dr. Naya Lekht, PhD

Dr. Lekht is a scholar, writer, and educator whose work examines the history of anti-Jewish movements, Soviet propaganda, Jewish identity, and contemporary antizionism. Her educational framework traces Jew-hatred across religion, race, and nation.

Her curriculum work grew from teaching in university and Jewish-school settings and identifying a gap in education about anti-Jewish movements before and after the Holocaust.

Executive Director

Natasha H. Pein

Natasha Pein is an educator, author, and strategic leader focused on Soviet antizionism, curriculum, institutional response, media narratives, and public education.

Her work includes educational resources, curriculum analysis, speaking, and leadership through STOPAZ and Pinnacle Integrity in Education for All.

Senior Behaviour Scientist

Dr. Brian Cugelman, PhD

Dr. Cugelman is an applied psychologist and digital strategist with experience in behaviour change, research, public engagement, and evidence-based program design.

He contributes expertise in persuasion, psychological resilience, communication, digital behaviour, and the design of educational and strategic tools.
