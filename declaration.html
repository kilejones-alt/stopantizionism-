Global Declaration

Antizionism is Jew-Hatred.

This declaration provides institutions and leaders with the language needed to identify, reject, and confront antizionist narratives.

Why it matters

Antizionism is not merely a political position. It functions as a comprehensive moral narrative that casts Jews, Zionism, and the Jewish state as uniquely illegitimate.

The declaration establishes a standard for education, policy, media, and public life while preserving the distinction between legitimate criticism of Israeli policy and ideological hostility to Jewish collective self-determination.

Define the ideology

State the meaning clearly and distinguish it from ordinary political disagreement.

Set a standard

Give organizations precise language for governance, training, and public communication.

Affirm equal rights

Recognize the Jewish people’s right to identity, belonging, and self-determination.

Guide action

Provide a basis for educational, institutional, and civic response.

Read and endorse the full declaration.
