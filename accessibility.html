Website information

Accessibility

STOPAZ aims to make its website usable by a broad range of visitors.

Our approach

The site uses semantic page structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, descriptive links, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Some archival images and externally hosted forms or media may have accessibility limitations that are outside the control of this static website.

Feedback

To report an accessibility problem, email info@stopaz.org and include the page address and a description of the issue.
