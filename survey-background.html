World Symposium Against Antizionism

Survey Background

Context for the anonymous follow-up survey supporting future symposiums, educational initiatives, and community engagement.

Why feedback matters

Evaluation strengthens future programming.

The World Symposium Against Antizionism brought together participants, experts, advocates, and community leaders for discussions on antizionism and contemporary antisemitism.

The follow-up survey evaluates content, speakers, subject matter, logistics, organization, and overall participant experience. STOPAZ states that the survey is anonymous and asks respondents not to include identifying information in open-ended answers.
