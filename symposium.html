The symposium

The symposium convenes scholars, educators, legal thinkers, public intellectuals, and advocates to examine antizionism as a modern form of Jew-hatred.

It brings research, education, law, and media analysis into a single institutional program.

Educational program

The Cycle of Libels and the Role of Token Jews.

A framework developed to help students recognize recurring patterns of demonization, identify manipulation, and develop the critical thinking and moral resilience needed to break the cycle before it reaches wider institutions.
