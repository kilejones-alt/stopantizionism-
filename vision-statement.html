Our Vision

A society able to recognize this era of Jew-hatred.

Historical literacy, moral integrity, and the courage to confront antizionism without euphemism.

The future STOPAZ works toward

Recognition must come before response.

STOPAZ envisions a world in which antizionism is broadly recognized as a contemporary form of Jew-hatred and understood as a threat to open societies, free inquiry, constitutional order, and civic trust.

The initiative teaches people to identify how antizionist narratives cloak themselves in the language of liberation, divide the world into villains, victims, and rescuers, and present ideological claims as unquestionable moral truths.

Vision in practice

Historical literacy joined to institutional courage.

Recognize the structure

Understand how moral language can be weaponized to demonize Jews, Zionism, and Israel.

Defend open societies

Connect the response to antizionism with the defense of free inquiry, individual dignity, and democratic norms.

Prepare public leaders

Equip educators, journalists, policymakers, and community leaders with coherent language and analytical tools.

Build durable coalitions

Support alliances grounded in evidence, historical understanding, and consistent moral standards.

STOPAZ
