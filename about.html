About us

Stop Antizionism is an educational, analytical, and advocacy initiative committed to exposing antizionism as the third and current era of Jew-hatred.

Antizionism recasts Jews, Israel, and Zionism through the moral vocabulary of the present. STOPAZ examines that ideological system and gives people the frameworks to recognize its narratives, inversions, libels, and methods of institutional spread.

The appearance of Jew-hatred is also an early warning sign of wider forces that weaken democratic societies. Defending Jews and defending the moral foundations of the free world are inseparable goals.
