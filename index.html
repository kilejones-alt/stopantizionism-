The three-era analysis

Stop Antizionism is an educational, analytical, and advocacy initiative committed to exposing antizionism as the third and current era of Jew-hatred.

Drawing on scholarship, historical analysis, and contemporary research, STOPAZ develops the language, curricula, and analytical resources required to identify and confront the antizionist worldview.
