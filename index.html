What STOPAZ does

Education built for the present era.

Stop Antizionism is an educational, analytical, and advocacy initiative. It connects historical scholarship with contemporary research so students, educators, professionals, and institutions can identify antizionist narratives and respond with clarity.

The work spans certificate training, research, institutional guidance, public programs, and a visual exhibition tracing Jew-hatred across three eras.
