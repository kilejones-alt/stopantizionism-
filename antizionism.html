The Exhibition

Antizionism

This room traces antizionism across Soviet, Arab, and Western contexts. Select an archive to view the image sequence.

Soviet

Propaganda, ideological framing, and disinformation supplied core antizionist language and imagery.

Arab

Regional political and religious anti-Zionist forms extended older anti-Jewish motifs into a modern political register.

Western

In universities, media, and protest culture, antizionism is recast through decolonization, race, and human-rights rhetoric.
